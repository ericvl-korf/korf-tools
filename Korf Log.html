    Match the log to the YouTube video (tap sync)

    You find a few events in the video and press Now when each one happens. Korf Log compares your taps with the logged times to find how far the log is from the video. Pause the video on the moment, then press Now: the tap is as exact as you are (about 1 to 2 seconds). Two to four events per half is enough, spread across the half so a drift shows up. Nothing is uploaded or read from the video.

      Works from the match video file on this computer; the video is not uploaded.

      Check the shot times against the video

      Check times looks at the ball around each shot and works out the exact time of the top of its flight. It uses the ball positions saved while you tracked and, for shots that have none, finds the ball in the video. Penalties and free passes are logged at the referee's call, so their time is that of the shot that follows. It then lists the shots whose logged time is off, with the time they would move to; untick any you do not want to move. Nothing changes until you press Apply changes.

      To keep the whole log in step with the video, press the orange Time out start and Time out end buttons whenever the clock is stopped: the video keeps running, and Korf Log adds the time out to the video time.
