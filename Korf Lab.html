Files picked by hand are kept in this browser for next time. Only team, event and seconds columns are required; the tracker's CSV export has the rest. JSON backups saved from Korf Log are converted when loaded.

Convert a Yellow Bucket export

Pick a Shot Location export (CSV) from Yellow Bucket and convert it into an event log that Korf Lab can read. Converted files give you shots, goals, shot %, shot types (open play, free pass, penalty), rebounds by team, shots by distance, goals by 5-minute block, score progression, the shot map, and shot counts for each named shooter. They have no passes, attacks, turnovers, substitutions, division or gender, so those views stay hidden.

Note: shot map markers are plotted on a simulated 40 × 20 m court, using each shot's distance and angle from the post.
